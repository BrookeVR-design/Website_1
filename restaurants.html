My Favorite Restaurants

Welcome to my list of my favorite restaurants in Athens, GA! They range from sit-down dinners to quick bites.

Last Resort Grill

Last Resort Grill is a local favorite for its delicious Southern cuisine and cozy atmosphere. The menu features a variety of dishes, including fried chicken, shrimp and grits, and catfish.

Address: 174 E Clayton St, Athens, GA 30601

Phone: (706) 543-1234

Website: www.lastresortgrill.com

Baddies Burgers

Baddies Burgers is a must-visit for burger lovers. They offer a variety of gourmet burgers made with fresh ingredients and unique toppings. The casual atmosphere and friendly staff make it a great spot for a quick meal.
