My Favorite Cafes

Welcome to my list of my favorite cafes in Athens, GA! They range from coffee shops to breakfast spots.

The Farm Cart

The Farm Cart is home to the best biscuits in Athens! My favorite to get is the 'the farmer' with a lavender chai to drink on the side.

Cafe Racer has the best donuts and chai lattes! They're a great breakfast/brunch spot to grab through the drive-thru or to sit down and eat the chorizo fries off their lunch menu.

Jittery Joe's

Jittery Joe's is an Athens staple. You can find them anywhere is be assured your drink is going to be good. I love the vanilla chai with an apple cinnamon muffin to go along with it.
